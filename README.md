# Mosswood Field Club

## Play online

[Play Mosswood Field Club](https://intrepion.github.io/kata-pokemon-clone-gpt-6-luna-extra-high/) — free, playable in your browser.

A small, original browser adventure about exploring a forest, meeting wild creatures, and inviting a few along. It borrows the familiar rhythm of a monster-catching RPG while using its own setting, creatures, pixel art, and names.

## Play

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. There is no build step, package install, or server to run. Your expedition saves automatically in the browser that you play in.

## Explore

- Walk with **WASD** or the **arrow keys**. The on-screen pad works on touch screens.
- Step into the tall grass to find wild creatures.
- Pick a move, use a capture sphere, or switch your active creature during a battle.
- Visit the little field hut and press **E** to heal your crew and restock capture spheres.
- Catch all six neighbors to fill your field guide.
- Use **Start over** to begin a fresh expedition.

The game runs entirely in the browser and stores its save in local storage. Clearing the browser's site data clears that save.
